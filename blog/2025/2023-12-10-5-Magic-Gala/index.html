The Tsinghua University Magic Gala is an annual event supported by ‘Tsinghua Student Club Development Program’ and organized by Tsinghua University Student Magic Club. I was honored to serve as both the executive director and a performer for the 5th Tsinghua University Magic Gala, Into the Wondrous Way (悟入奇途). The gala was narrated from the perspective of ‘Mr. Rabbit’ and featured 14 magic shows and 1 singing performance, with a duration of approximately 2 hours and an audience of around 250.
