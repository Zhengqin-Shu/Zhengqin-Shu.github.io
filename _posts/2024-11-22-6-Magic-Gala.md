The Tsinghua University Magic Gala is an annual event supported by 'Tsinghua Student Club Development Program' and organized by Tsinghua University Student Magic Club. I was honored to serve as chief stage manager of the 6th Tsinghua University Magic Gala, <strong><em> Marvels and Oddities </em></strong> (光怪陆离). The gala featured 13 magic shows, with a duration of approximately 2 hours and an audience of around 250. Click [here](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1iRcDewEEj/?spm_id_from=333.1387.homepage.video_card.click) for the full recording of the gala.
