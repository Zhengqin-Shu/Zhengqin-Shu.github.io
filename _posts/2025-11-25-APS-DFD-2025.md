With the support from Prof. H. Jane Bae and DFD Travel Award, I was honored to present at the American Physical Society 78th Annual Meeting of the Division of Fluid Dynamics ([APS DFD 2025](https://www.aps.org/events/2025/78th-dfd-annual-meeting)), Houston. During the interactive session, we shared our research on the <em> Analogous dynamics of Couette-Poiseuille flows and adverse-pressure-gradient turbulent boundary layers</em> and received valuable feedback and suggestions. Click [here](https://archive.aps.org/dfd/2025/e27/5/) for our abstract.
