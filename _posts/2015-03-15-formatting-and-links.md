I was honored to perform my original 20-minute magic show, <strong><em> Departure </em></strong>, at Tsinghua’s 2024 New Year Gala, in front of an audience of over 2,000. In the show, I invited a random volunteer on stage and asked them to name their ideal travel destination. On the giant “prediction” boarding pass, their name and chosen destination were already printed! Click [here](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1PZ421U7b8/?spm_id_from=333.1387.homepage.video_card.click) for the full performance video.
